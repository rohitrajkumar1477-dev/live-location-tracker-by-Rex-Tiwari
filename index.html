📱 Phone kho jaye ya chori ho to

  1. Google Find My Device kholo — google.com/android/find — wahi Gmail ID se login karo jo phone me hai. Wahan se phone ko ring, lock ya erase kar sakte ho.
  2. IMEI block karwao: ceir.gov.in par chori hue phone ka IMEI block karwao — uske baad wo phone India me kisi bhi SIM par nahi chalega.
  3. Police complaint karo aur is dashboard ka screenshot + IMEI number saath le jao.
  4. Carrier (Jio / Airtel / Vi) ko call karke SIM band karwao.

Sirf apne ya family ke phone par, unki permission se use karo. Kisi aur ka phone bina bataye track karna galat hai.
